How it works · B2B integration

How a draw actually happens.

A partner lender calls a single endpoint to draw against their facility. Moodeng handles authorization, the USDC draw, compliance filing, FX conversion, and disbursement — the lender gets local currency in the borrower's hand and an on-chain repayment record back.

B2B · Vault

Investors earn from what borrowers actually repay — not token emissions.
