Questions before you trust us

The honest answers.

You should understand the whole loop before you borrow. If an answer still feels vague, message us.

Who funds the loan?

A person chooses your request from the Moodeng request board. Moodeng does not automatically approve or fund every ask. If no one funds it before it expires, you owe nothing.

Where does the extra $3 go?

In the $15 to $18 example, the $3 is the funder’s expected return for helping. Moodeng adds no service fee or processing fee. Every request shows its exact expected payback before it goes live.

Why does the app say USDC?

USDC is the digital-dollar format the loan uses inside the app. The cash-out guide shows how to move it to pesos through Moneybees or another available external provider.

What happens if I am late?

The amount does not grow, but the late payment becomes part of the record future funders see. Message us before the due date if the week has changed. We do not contact your family or shame you.

What information do funders see?

They see the request terms, the reason you chose to share, your verification status, and your Moodeng repayment record. They do not see your identity documents, contacts, photos, or messages.

How quickly can a request be funded?

Many funded requests receive help within a few hours, but timing varies and funding is never guaranteed. The request simply expires if nobody chooses it.
