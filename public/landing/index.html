Before you borrow — from anyone

Five questions to ask any loan app.

Including us. If an app can’t answer these in plain words, close it. Here’s where we stand on each:

Will the number grow while I sleep? Here: never. $18 today is $18 on the due date. No rollover, no overnight penalty.
Who can see my contacts? No one. We never ask for access to your phone. Funders see your request and your record — never your people.
What exactly do I owe, and when? One number, one date — on the screen before you confirm, and neither ever moves.
Where does the extra money go? To the person who funded you — their thank-you for helping. Our cut is $0.
What happens if I’m late? A human messages you and we figure it out together. No charges pile on, no calls to your family.

Screenshot these five. Ask them anywhere you borrow — you deserve straight answers.

Common questions

Questions people ask us.

What happens if I can’t pay on time?

Message us — a real person answers, and we’ll figure it out together. What we never do: call your contacts, add surprise charges, or shame you.

Who is actually giving me the money?

Real people. Someone read your request and chose to help — not a company, not an algorithm.

If you don’t charge fees, how do you make money?

The extra you pay back — like $3 on a $15 loan — goes to the person who funded you, as their thank-you for helping. Moodeng adds nothing on top of it: no service fee, no processing fee, no cut of your loan. That’s the whole model, and it’s on the screen before you say yes.

Is this legit? Who’s behind Moodeng?

Real people with names and faces — we watched loan sharks and predatory loan apps hurt people we love, and built the opposite. Every borrower is one verified real person, every loan is on the record, and you can message us any time and a human answers. Read our story.

How do I turn it into pesos?

When the money lands, you can move it into pesos through apps you already use, like GCash — and move it back the same way when you repay.

Why does the app say “USDC”?

USDC is a digital dollar — built to stay worth 1 US dollar, and you can check the peso value on screen before every move. It’s the form your loan arrives in, and it’s why our fees are $0: digital dollars move without banks taking a cut along the way. You turn it into pesos through apps like GCash — and the whole amount arrives. (The one tiny fee in the loop — a cent or two — happens when you repay, and it’s the converter’s, not ours.)

Why does it start at only $15?

Starting small is how trust gets built on both sides. Every on-time repayment raises your limit — $15 → $20 → $40 → $60+.
