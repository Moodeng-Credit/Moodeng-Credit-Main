The complete money loop

USDC out. Local cash access. USDC back.

Borrowers can cash out through familiar Philippine routes, then use those same kinds of services to buy USDC for repayment. Moodeng keeps the loan and its outcome tied to the borrower’s record.

  1. 01You fund USDC

    You choose a single request and fund from your wallet on Base.

  2. 02They access pesos

    Supported routes include Coins.ph, GCrypto, PDAX, and external providers.

  3. 03They add USDC to repay

    The app shows the Base address, provider route, amount due, and payment options.

  4. 04Your wallet receives it

    Repayment is sent automatically to the wallet you funded from. No claim step.

Moodeng charges $0 to repay. External providers can charge their own conversion rate, network cost, or service fee, and route availability varies by location.

The actual lender journey

Four decisions. Real screens.

Moodeng does not choose a request for you. You inspect the terms and record, then decide whether the amount and risk fit.

The lender view
  1. 01 · BROWSE

    Compare the open requests.

    Start with the reason, requested amount, expected return, and due date. Nothing is automatically allocated; you choose which request deserves a closer look.

  2. 02 · REVIEW

    Read the record, including the misses.

    Open the borrower profile to see verification, credit level, completed loans, late outcomes, and defaults. The record is context, not a guarantee.

  3. 03 · FUND

    Choose one request yourself.

    Return to the board, open the request, and fund only an amount you are comfortable putting at risk. Moodeng does not promise the expected return.

  4. 04 · FOLLOW

    The outcome returns to the record.

    On time, late, partial, or defaulted, the result remains visible in Moodeng. That history gives the next lender more evidence than the last one had.

Questions lenders ask

Four direct answers.

The important mechanics, without the sales language.

Is repayment guaranteed?

No. A borrower can pay late, repay partially, or default. Only fund an amount you can afford to lose.

How does a borrower repay?

They add USDC through a wallet or supported local service, then choose an early, partial, or full payment. External service fees can apply; Moodeng’s repayment fee is $0.

How do I receive repayment?

If the borrower repays, USDC is sent automatically to the wallet you funded from. You do not need to return to Moodeng and claim it.

What does identity verification prove?

It confirms the account passed a supported identity check. It does not prove income, affordability, or future repayment, and lenders do not receive the borrower’s identity documents.

Why does the borrower have an incentive to repay?

Clean repayment builds the borrower’s Moodeng record and can unlock the next limit. Late, partial, and defaulted outcomes stay visible; default freezes the account.

What should I check before I fund?

Read the requested amount, expected payback and date, identity-verification status, current balance, credit level, reason, and every visible repayment outcome. First-time borrowers have less evidence, so their limits start small.
