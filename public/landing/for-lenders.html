“Expected back” is the borrower’s agreed repayment amount — not a promise from Moodeng. There’s no insurance fund. Only fund an amount you can afford to lose.
What happens on a default: the account is frozen, the miss goes on the record every future lender sees, and — because every borrower is one verified person — there’s no starting over with a fresh account. What never happens: harassment.

Common questions

Questions lenders ask us.

Is this charity or investing?

Neither. It’s person-to-person lending: you help someone through a tight week, and they pay back what they agreed — a little more than they borrowed. If you want to grow savings, this isn’t it. If you want your money to mean something and come back, it is.

What’s the track record?

As of July 2026: 102 verified borrowers, 94% repaid on time, every outcome on a public record. We’re early; we’d rather show small real numbers than big invented ones.

What do I actually earn?

Each request sets its own expected payback — fund $15, expected back $18 is typical, but amounts and paybacks vary by request. No APR, no compounding: the payback you see when you fund is the payback the borrower agreed to. Repayment is not guaranteed.

What happens if someone doesn’t repay?

You can lose what you lent — we won’t pretend otherwise. Every borrower is verified as one real person (no fresh accounts after a default), and a miss freezes their borrowing and goes on their record. What we don’t do: harass anyone.

How do I put money in?

With a debit card, right in the app — or digital dollars you already have. Funding a small request takes about a minute.

When do I get paid back?

Each request shows its expected repayment date before you fund, and the app shows the loan’s status along the way. In practice most repayments land on payday — but late and partial repayments happen, and the record shows them.

How do I choose well?

Read the reason, then the record. Repaid loans and on-time history show you how someone behaves. First-timers have no record yet — their requests are capped small, so no single yes costs much.
