How risk works

We’re as straight with you as we are with them.

Borrowers get no hidden charges. You get no hidden risk.

Repayment isn’t guaranteed. Most people pay back — but if a borrower doesn’t, you can lose what you lent. There’s no insurance fund behind this. It’s a real loan to a real person.
That’s why everyone starts at $15. New borrowers can’t ask for more, so no single yes costs much — and limits only grow through repayments that actually happened. Never fund more than you’d be okay losing.
What happens on a default: the borrower’s account is frozen, the miss goes on the record every future lender sees, and because every borrower is verified as one real person, they can’t start over with a fresh account. What never happens: harassment. That’s a line we don’t cross, even for you.
This is helping, with your money back at the end. Not a savings account, not a get-rich app. The extra few dollars are a thank-you for trusting someone when the bank wouldn’t.

Common questions

Questions lenders ask us.

Is this charity or investing?

Neither, really. It’s person-to-person lending: you help someone through a tight week, and they pay back what they promised — a little more than they borrowed. If you’re looking for a place to grow savings, this isn’t it. If you want your money to mean something to a real person and come back to you, it is.

What’s the track record?

As of July 2026: 102 verified borrowers, 94% repaid on time, and every repayment on a public record. Last month’s loans were all repaid — most a day or two before the deadline, because repayment is timed to payday. We’re early and the numbers are small; we’d rather show you small real numbers than big invented ones.

What do I actually earn?

Each loan has one flat payback the borrower sets upfront — fund $15, get back $18 is typical. No APR, no compounding, no rollover: what you see when you fund is exactly what comes back. Whether you re-lend it is up to you.

What happens if someone doesn’t repay?

You can lose what you lent — we won’t pretend otherwise. What we do: every borrower is verified as one real person (no fresh accounts after a default), everyone starts at $15, and a missed repayment freezes their borrowing and goes on the record every future lender sees. What we don’t do: harass anyone. That’s a line we don’t cross even for you.

How do I put money in?

With a debit card, right in the app — or by moving digital dollars you already have. Funding a $15 request takes about a minute.

When do I get paid back?

On or before the due date the borrower set — you’ll see the exact date before you fund, and the app shows you every loan’s status along the way. In practice most repayments land on payday, often a day or two early.

How do I choose well?

Read the reason, then the record. A borrower with repaid loans and an on-time history is showing you how they behave. First-timers have no record yet — that’s what the $15 cap is for: it’s the size of a first hello, not a leap of faith.
