The whole journey

From a request to a record you can build on.

Each step gives you the terms or status you need. No step depends on a sales promise from the one before it.

01

Ask on your terms.

Set the amount, expected payback, date, and reason. The first amount is $15. Nothing changes after the request goes live.

Start a request →
02

A real person decides.

Your request appears on the board with its exact terms. A funder reads it and chooses whether to help. If nobody funds it before it expires, you owe nothing.

03

Move digital dollars to pesos.

A funded loan lands as USDC in your Base Account. The app shows available routes to pesos, plus the timing and provider details for each.

Open the cash-out guide →
04

Repay in parts or full.

Pay early, in parts, or all at once. Completed loans and setbacks stay on your Moodeng record, giving future funders more history to review.

Open the repay guide →

The part most loan pages hide

Funding can take time, or not happen.

Moodeng requests are funded by people, not an automatic approval system. Many funded requests receive help within a few hours, but no request is guaranteed. If yours expires unfunded, you owe nothing and can decide what to do next.
