How it works

Four steps. Real screens.

This is the actual app — so you can see exactly what happens before you sign up for anything.

1

Ask for what you need.

You set the amount, what you'll pay back, the date, and the reason. Nothing is decided for you — and nothing changes after you ask. First time? You prove you're one real person, once — that's the whole sign-up.

The actual request form in the app.

2

Real people see it — and fund it.

Your request goes on the board next to the others. A real person reads your reason and chooses to help — not a company, not an algorithm. It's not instant — and asking is risk-free: if no one funds it by your date, the request simply expires and you owe nothing.

The actual request board — sample requests shown.

3

Turn it into pesos.

The money arrives as USDC — digital dollars, each one always worth one US dollar — in your own Base Account, protected by our partner Coinbase. Move them into pesos through apps you already use, like GCash — and back the same way when you repay.

The actual cash-out screen in the app.

4

Keep your word — your limit grows.

Every on-time repayment builds a record that belongs to you — and lenders can see it, the good and the setbacks. That's why no credit history is needed to start.
