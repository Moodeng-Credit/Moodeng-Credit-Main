Common questions

Cash-out questions, answered.

What if I don’t have GCash?

GCash is the most popular route, but not the only one — Coins.ph, PDAX, and Binance work too. Pick whichever you already use; the app shows what each route takes before you choose.

Who charges the conversion fee?

The converter — the app doing the pesos exchange — and it’s very small. It’s their fee, not ours: Moodeng adds nothing on top. You see the honest total before you confirm.

Why does the app say “USDC”?

USDC is the official name of the digital dollars your loan travels as — 1 USDC is always worth 1 US dollar. It’s also why our fees are $0: digital dollars move without banks taking a cut along the way.
