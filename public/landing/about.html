Our story

We built the loan we wished existed.

Moodeng began with a simple refusal: a small loan should never grow without warning, expose someone’s private life, or turn a hard week into public shame.

Read why we started ↓See what we built

Where it started

We watched small debt do large damage.

The worst credit products do not look complicated. They start with a small amount, then use a growing price, pressure, and shame to make it impossible to leave.

01 · The street lender

A small loan kept growing.

In Thailand and the Philippines, people we know faced the same pattern: borrow a little, owe much more the next week, then owe more again when the first due date becomes impossible.

02 · The app version

Technology made the pressure faster.

Some loan apps replaced the collector at the door with access to contacts, family, coworkers, and public messages. The interface looked cleaner, but the fear was the same.

03 · A different rail

Digital dollars changed what a tiny loan could cost to move.

USDC and Base made it possible to move small amounts through a global digital-dollar network without building the experience around bank transfer overhead. That created room for a person-to-person model with no Moodeng service fee.

04 · Moodeng

One request. One clear deal. One record.

A borrower chooses the reason, expected payback, and date. A person chooses whether to fund it. The outcome stays on a record the borrower can build, without giving Moodeng access to a private phone.

The rules we keep

Warm words only matter when the product has hard boundaries.

These are not campaign promises. They shape what Moodeng asks for, what funders see, and how the team responds when repayment is difficult.

  • ✓The full expected payback is visible firstNo hidden Moodeng service or processing fee.
  • ✓Private phones stay privateNo contacts, photos, or messages.
  • ✓Every outcome stays on the recordCompleted loans, late payments, and defaults remain visible.
  • ✓Collection never becomes humiliationNo public shame or contact-list pressure.
