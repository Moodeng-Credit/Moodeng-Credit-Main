Our story

We built the loan we wished existed.

Moodeng began with a simple refusal: a small loan should never grow without warning, expose someone’s private life, or turn a hard week into public shame.

Read why we started ↓See what we built

Where it started

We watched small debt do large damage.

The worst credit products do not look complicated. They start with a small amount, then use a growing price, pressure, and shame to make it impossible to leave.

01 · The street lender

A small loan kept growing.

Around here everyone knows the deal: borrow small, owe big by next week — and the number keeps growing until the collector stops coming. In Thailand and the Philippines we watched it eat people we know. Not statistics. Neighbors.

The corner deal Borrow ₱1,000 owe ₱1,200 next week — and it grows every week you can’t pay

02 · Why it’s personal

Emma watched it happen at home.

Emma grew up in Thailand, where the sharks run the same playbook they run everywhere — a small loan, a number that grows every week, then the collectors and the shame. She watched it push people she loves into depression, and she lost friends to it. Not to the debt itself — to what the debt did to them. That grief is what Moodeng is built from: the belief that a loan should never be able to do that to a person.

Emma “Honest, fair loans that build you up — so nobody ever needs the corner deal again. That’s the whole mission.”

03 · The app version

The “better way” was worse.

Loan apps promised to replace the sharks. Instead they scraped contacts and shamed families and coworkers when payments ran late. And the pricing trick: “0% interest” in the ad, with the real cost hidden as a “service fee” so interest caps don’t apply. The interface looked cleaner, but the fear was the same.

The ad said 0% interest! the receipt said: + ₱500 “service fee” — same shark, new clothes

04 · A different rail

Then money itself got better.

Digital dollars (USDC on Base) changed the math: money that moves anywhere, in minutes, for basically nothing. No banks in the middle taking a cut — so a loan service doesn’t need fees to survive. The excuse every “service fee” hid behind was gone.

Sending money, now $15 → $15 what leaves is what arrives. fee: $0

05 · Moodeng

One request. One clear deal. One record.

Small amounts. One number you owe, on the screen before you say yes — and it never moves. Funded by real people who chose your request. No fee from our side, and we never touch your contacts, so nobody can call your family. The opposite of everything we watched.

The rules we keep

Warm words only matter when the product has hard boundaries.

These are not campaign promises. They shape what Moodeng asks for, what funders see, and how the team responds when repayment is difficult.

  • ✓The full expected payback is visible firstNo hidden Moodeng service or processing fee.
  • ✓Private phones stay privateNo contacts, photos, or messages.
  • ✓Every outcome stays on the recordCompleted loans, late payments, and defaults remain visible.
  • ✓Collection never becomes humiliationNo public shame or contact-list pressure.
