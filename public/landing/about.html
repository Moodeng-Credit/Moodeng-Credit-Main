Our story

We hated what borrowing looked like. So we changed it.

Moodeng exists because of what we watched happen to people we know — and because the technology finally arrived to do it right.

Where it started

We watched the sharks work.

Around here everyone knows the deal: borrow small, owe big by next week — and the number keeps growing until the collector stops coming. We watched it eat people we know. Not statistics. Neighbors.

The corner deal
Borrow ₱1,000
owe ₱1,200 next week — and it grows every week you can’t pay

Then came the apps

The “better way” was worse.

Loan apps promised to replace the sharks. Instead they scraped your contacts and shamed your family when you were late. Whole communities online exist just for the victims. And the pricing trick: “0% interest” in the ad — with the real cost hidden as a “service fee”, so the law’s interest caps don’t apply.

The ad said
0% interest!
the receipt said: + ₱500 “service fee” — same shark, new clothes

The turn

Then money itself got better.

Digital dollars (USDC) changed the math: money that moves anywhere, in minutes, for basically nothing. No banks in the middle taking a cut — which means a loan service doesn’t need fees to survive. The excuse every “service fee” hid behind was gone.

Sending money, now
$15 → $15
what leaves is what arrives. fee: $0

So we built it

The loan we wished existed.

Small amounts. One number you owe, on the screen before you say yes — and it never moves. Funded by real people who chose your request. No fees from our side, ever. And we never touch your contacts, so nobody can call your family. The opposite of everything we watched.
