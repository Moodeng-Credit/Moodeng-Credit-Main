Call booking bot — try it

Pretend you're a borrower who just connected Messenger. Type when you're free, pick a time, answer the questions, then confirm. It uses our live free times and real DeepSeek AI, but nothing here books a real call or sends anything on Messenger.

What to try (about 10 minutes)
  1. A normal booking: type "tomorrow 4 pm", tap the ⭐ time, answer as a working parent with a small business, then type YES and a first name. Check the "Before the call" box on the right.
  2. Messy typing: "bukas after 6pm", "pwede po ba sa Lunes ng hapon?", "kahit kailan basta gabi", "ok". Does it understand, or ask again?
  3. A taken or full time: "saturday 2pm". Is it clear the time is taken and which times are closest?
  4. Someone we can't help yet: answer "Between jobs", "Just myself", "None right now". You should see "our team reviews every booking", then a kind decline.
  5. Tight money: answer "I run short", "5-6 or a lending app", "Not sure". It should still book, but the box says ⚠️ Talk first.
  6. Filipino: tap FIL at the top of the chat and do one booking in Filipino.

Anything confusing, too long, or worded wrong? Write it down with the exact message, and send it to George. Use "Start over" at the bottom right to begin again.
