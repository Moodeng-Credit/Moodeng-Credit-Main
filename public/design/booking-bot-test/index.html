Call booking bot — try it

Pretend you're a borrower who just connected Messenger. Type when you're free, pick a time, answer the questions, then confirm. It uses our live free times and real DeepSeek AI, but nothing here books a real call or sends anything on Messenger.

What to try (about 10 minutes)
  1. A normal booking: type "tomorrow 4 pm", tap the ⭐ time, answer as a working parent with a small business, tap 👍 Got it on the "$15 first loan" message, then type YES and a first name. Check the "Before the call" box on the right.
  2. Messy typing: "bukas after 6pm", "pwede po ba sa Lunes ng hapon?", "kahit kailan basta gabi", "ok". Does it understand, or ask again?
  3. A taken or full time: "saturday 2pm". Is it clear the time is taken and which times are closest?
  4. Someone in a tough spot: answer "Between jobs" and "None right now". They still book; the box on the right says ❗ Needs a closer look. Single people ("Just myself") are never marked down.
  5. Tight money: answer "I run short" and "5-6 or a lending app". It should still book, but the box says ⚠️ Talk first.
  6. Dodging: tap "Other" (first question), "Prefer not to say", or Skip on a type-it question (typing "secret" or "ayoko" counts too). The bot asks once more. Do it again: the bot says sorry, we work with people who know their situation, and books no call. Typing a day and time starts again. "Not sure" on when they could repay is a normal answer.
  7. After booking: type "ok", "thanks" or "can I bring a friend?". The bot says you're all set and keeps the booking. Typing a new day and time offers new times, with a 👍 Keep my time button; "cancel" cancels.
  8. Who they are: switch "Pretend borrower" (under the calendar). Maria's app bio already has her work, income and payday, so the chat skips those. Siti is in Indonesia: Jakarta time and Rupiah. Ana is a Filipino on Bangkok time.
  9. Filipino: tap FIL at the top of the chat and do one booking in Filipino.

Anything confusing, too long, or worded wrong? Write it down with the exact message, and send it to George. Use "Start over" at the bottom right to begin again.
