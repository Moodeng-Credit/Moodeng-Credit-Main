Design brief · Repayment flow · For Anna

Moodeng Repay Redesign

Hi Anna. This page walks through how a borrower repays a loan in Moodeng today, one screen at a time. Every screenshot is from the live app. Orange numbered pins point at the parts we talk about next to each screen.

The problem in one line: our repayment screens were designed by AI alone and they are confusing. Our new dashboard looks much better. We want repayment to feel like the dashboard: simpler, cuter and friendlier. Tap any screen to see it full size.

01 · The ask

Make repaying feel like the new dashboard

Who repays

Borrowers in the Philippines on their phones, often inside the Facebook or Messenger browser. Many are new to crypto. They borrowed a small amount in USDC (a digital dollar) and have to send it back before the due date.

What goes wrong

  • Too much on one long screen.
  • The Pay button is hidden in the bottom bar.
  • It looks nothing like the rest of the app.
  • People don't know which step they are on.

What we want

  • Same look and feel as the dashboard.
  • One clear thing to do on each screen.
  • Cute and warm: Moodeng the hippo, rewards, a real celebration at the end.
  • Less text, bigger buttons.

05 · What's confusing

The main problems, most important first

  1. The Pay button is in the wrong place. The amount is in the middle of the page but the button that pays it is a circle inside the bottom menu. When it appears, the "Repay" tab disappears and labels get cut off ("Dashbo", "listory").
  2. The first thing people see is homework, not their loan. A big "Add funds" card with 3 steps opens by itself. How much they owe, and when, is pushed down about three phone screens.
  3. Everything is on one long page. Choose a source, copy an address, read a warning, open another app, check fees, watch a progress bar, pick an amount, pay. No sense of "you are on step 2 of 4".
  4. The amount changes by itself. It fills in with whatever USDC is in the wallet ($18.40 here), not what they owe. People think they are paying the full loan.
  5. It feels scary. A red "lost forever" warning and lots of crypto words (USDC, Base, network, wallet address) come before any friendly reassurance.
  6. The ending is flat. Paying off a loan is the best moment in the app (points, a higher limit), but the success screen is a plain white card.
  7. It doesn't look like Moodeng. No hippo, different purples, different buttons, many tiny text sizes.

06 · Rules

Things the new design must keep

You can change how these look and where they go. They can't be removed.

Money safety

  • The Base network warning must be impossible to miss at the moment people copy their wallet address. Sending on the wrong network loses the money forever.
  • Wallet address shown short (0x1234…ABcd), one tap to copy, clear "Copied" feedback.
  • Once a payment is sent it can't be cancelled. Cancel is only possible before that.
  • People can never pay more than they owe.

Loan rules

  • Partial payments are allowed (25%, 50%, 75%, any amount).
  • Overdue loans: paying part of it lowers the debt but the account stays restricted until the loan is fully paid. This message must stay.
  • Show the due date and time left. Red when under 24 hours or overdue.
  • Show the Pandesal points they'll earn for paying on time.

Money sources

  • Coins.ph is the recommended option. Moneybees, GCrypto and PDAX are also offered. Binance only for people outside the Philippines.
  • Moodeng charges no fee. The other app charges a small fee, so people should send a little extra.
  • Moneybees is an outside service. We must say Moodeng isn't part of that deal.

App basics

  • Mobile first, 390px wide. Works in the Facebook in-app browser.
  • English and Filipino. Filipino text runs about 30% longer, so leave room.
  • The bottom menu (Request Board, Repay, Dashboard, History, Account) stays across the app.
  • Keep the "Watch how to repay" video and a "Get help" link.

07 · Deliverables

What we'd love back in Figma

Screens

  • Repay start: which loan, how much, when it's due
  • Top-up guide (when the wallet is short): source, copy address, send, waiting
  • Money arrived
  • Choose amount and pay (button next to the amount)
  • Paying / confirming
  • Paid part of the loan
  • Loan fully repaid, with points and level up
  • Overdue loan version
  • Empty states: no loans yet, finish setup, verify, add wallet

How

  • Reuse the dashboard's components and colors where possible.
  • A clickable prototype of the main path (short on funds → top up → pay in full → celebrate).
  • Ideas for the hippo's moods during repayment are very welcome. The dashboard already has "waiting", "loan" and "repaid" moods.
  • Screens we didn't capture are listed at the end of the step-by-step section.
