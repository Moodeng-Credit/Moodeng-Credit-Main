How people sign up and get in

Most borrowers arrive from a Facebook post and open Moodeng inside Facebook's own browser, where Google sign-in can't work. In the 30 days before this change, 26 people opened sign-up inside Facebook and only 5 got an account. Now they sign up with just their email, no code, and confirm it later, once they're approved.

Email only to start No code until approved Confirm by tapping the email Then out of Facebook for KYC
1 Sign up (email)→2 Onboarding→3 Connect Facebook→4 Video call→5 Approved: confirm email + password→6 ID check in Chrome→7 Loan

1 · Sign up inside Facebook

One field, no code, no password. Behind the scenes it's a guest account that keeps the email they typed but can't sign in with it yet. Supabase emails them a confirmation link and code straight away, but they don't need to open it now.

3 · Connect Facebook, then reminders

Step 1 is required: Messenger is how we reach them, and anyone stuck later can message us. Step 2 is payment reminders: phone notifications where they work, otherwise the WhatsApp card. Confirming a WhatsApp number already works; the reminder messages start once Meta approves them (next tab).

5 · Approved after the call: confirm email, leave Facebook

This screen covers the app once they're approved, before the ID check. Inside Facebook it tells them to tap the button in our email: that opens Moodeng in Chrome or Safari, already signed in to the same account, so the ID check runs outside Facebook. Typing the code also works.

Until the email is confirmed, the database refuses loans and payments from guest accounts, and the ID check won't start. If Facebook's browser forgets them, the link in the email signs them back into the same account from any browser.

Logging in

Phone codes (SMS) are paused for everyone until the "MOODENG" sender name is approved, so these screens are email only.

What changes: nothing on the screens people already see. Once Meta approves the three reminder messages and SendPulse has funds, one switch (borrower_whatsapp_enabled) starts sending them. Borrowers who confirmed WhatsApp get them; replying STOP turns them off.

Reminders on

Same card as today; once sending is on, it promises something that actually arrives.

The three messages

Utility messages in English, each with a "Repay now" button to moodeng.app/repay. "Due tomorrow" goes out only if no phone notification landed. Shown with sample values.

Due tomorrow · moodeng_due_tomorrow
Hi Juan, your Moodeng loan of 20 USDC is due tomorrow, 8:00 AM. Repay on time to keep growing your credit level.
Repay now
Due today · moodeng_due_today
Hi Juan, your Moodeng loan of 20 USDC is due today (8:00 AM). Tap below to repay.
Repay now
Overdue · moodeng_overdue
Hi Juan, your Moodeng loan of 20 USDC is overdue. Repay now to protect your credit level, or reply here if you need help.
Repay now

To turn it on

1. Meta approves the 3 messages (WhatsApp Manager → Message templates says Approved). 2. Add about $5 in SendPulse, with auto-recharge off. 3. Flip borrower_whatsapp_enabled to true. 4. Test from a second phone: send the code, receive a reminder, send STOP.
