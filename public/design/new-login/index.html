New sign-up and log-in screens

Built for people who open Moodeng from a Facebook or Messenger link. Last month about 40 of them opened sign-up inside Facebook and never got in, mostly because Google sign-in doesn't work there. Now they sign up with a Philippine phone number or an email, get a code, and never need a password.

No password 🇵🇭 Phone or email Works inside Facebook Same account in Chrome later

Inside Facebook or Messenger

One field. Filipinos start on their phone number; anyone can switch to email. Google is a small "open in Chrome" line instead of a button that fails.

The code screen

Texts come from "SEMAPHORE" (our SMS company) until the "Moodeng" sender name is approved in 3–5 days, so the screen says so up front.

After they're in

New accounts get one question to avoid duplicates. Phone sign-ups add an email later, without leaving Facebook. A password is always optional.

In a normal browser (Chrome)

Nothing removed. Two new buttons let anyone who signed up inside Facebook log in from Chrome with a code.
